# Voluntary Support

Valid Blockchain is independently designed, developed, and maintained by a solo open-source developer.

If this project is useful to you, if you believe in independent infrastructure, or if you simply want to support the work, you may make a voluntary donation.

Support is never required to use, evaluate, fork, contribute to, or discuss this project.

## What Donations Support

Voluntary donations help sustain the practical work required to build and maintain the project, including:

- Development time
- Testing and debugging
- Testnet and private-network infrastructure
- Technical audits
- Hardware, hosting, domains, and operational expenses
- Documentation, examples, and deployment guides
- Release engineering and maintenance
- Multi-node and adversarial testing
- Security hardening and future independent review
- Continued open-source development of the Valid ecosystem

Donations support the maintainer's ability to continue doing the work. They are not payment for a product, financial instrument, or special relationship with the project.

## What Donations Do Not Provide

A voluntary donation does **not** provide, promise, create, or imply:

- Tokens, coins, VLid allocations, airdrops, or future token claims
- Equity, ownership, shares, revenue rights, or investment rights
- Any expectation of profit, return, appreciation, yield, or financial benefit
- Governance authority, voting rights, protocol control, or special influence
- Priority access, early access, private builds, premium features, or exclusive releases
- Priority support, consulting, implementation work, or guaranteed responses
- A security guarantee, audit guarantee, uptime guarantee, warranty, or service-level agreement
- A contractual relationship, employment relationship, partnership, agency relationship, or fiduciary relationship
- Control over project direction, roadmap decisions, licensing, protocol rules, or maintainership
- Access to private user data, private network data, internal infrastructure, or nonpublic project materials
- Immunity from moderation, enforcement of project rules, or normal community standards
- Any obligation by the maintainer beyond continuing to act in good faith as an independent open-source developer

No person or entity acquires special status within the project by donating.

## No Investment or Sale

Donations are voluntary support for independent open-source development.

They are **not** an investment, token sale, securities offering, presale, crowdfunding purchase, prepayment for future services, or solicitation of financial speculation. Do not donate with an expectation of financial return, token value, access rights, control, or any other benefit.

Any future software, protocol, token, network, or ecosystem feature will be governed by its own published technical documentation, software license, protocol rules, and applicable terms. It will not be governed by a donation.

## Independence

Valid is independently maintained.

The maintainer retains responsibility for project direction, technical decisions, release timing, roadmap priorities, security decisions, community rules, and maintenance practices. Donations are appreciated support; they do not purchase control of the project.

The project may change direction, delay features, discontinue features, alter its roadmap, or cease development. Open-source software is provided under its applicable license and without guarantees beyond those expressly stated in that license.

## Transparency

When practical, development updates may describe work completed, current priorities, and major infrastructure or testing needs.

These updates are provided for transparency. They do not create a contractual duty to deliver a particular feature, timeline, release, or outcome.
